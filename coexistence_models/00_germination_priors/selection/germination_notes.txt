Germination priors created for TRCY and TROR.

Interpretation:
- PJ uses direct row-level Perenjori field evidence.
- BEN uses direct row-level Kunjin field evidence as the Bendering analogue.
- Hao Ran rows are excluded.
- Zero-germination rows are retained because they are valid binomial observations unless known to be failed trials.
- Main germination fraction is field_number_germinants / field_number_seeds.
- Nonviable seeds are retained in the denominator, so this estimates realised germination from total field seeds, not germination conditional on viability.
- Lost seeds are retained in the denominator in the main analysis; this is conservative and should ideally be checked with a sensitivity analysis.
- GH, NAM, and CS use weakly informed priors centered on pooled PJ+BEN evidence.
